Did Your Dog or Cat Chew a Sago Palm? Check What to Do.

Sago palms and their relatives are among the most dangerous plants for pets. Tell us what happened to get your next step.

Last reviewed November 27, 2026 by PetPoisonCalc against the sources listed below.

Every part of a sago palm is toxic, and the seeds are the worst. One or two seeds can be fatal.

Sago palms (Cycas) and related cycads like coontie and cardboard palm contain cycasin, which attacks the liver. Pets can look fine for hours before signs start. This checker does not do dose math, because no safe amount exists. Call poison control or an emergency vet now.

Emergency Vet or Poison Control: Which Do I Call?

Early treatment matters most. A vet may make your pet vomit, give activated charcoal, protect the liver, and check liver values for several days.

Go Straight to an Emergency Vet If…

  • Your pet chewed any part of a sago palm, coontie, or cardboard palm
  • Your pet is vomiting, has diarrhea, or seems weak
  • You see yellow gums or eyes, or unusual bruising
  • Your pet is wobbly or has a seizure

Call Poison Control First If…

  • You are on the way and want guidance
  • You are not sure the plant is a sago palm
  • You want help identifying the plant from a photo
  • You want a case number to hand to your vet
Do not try to make your pet vomit at home unless a veterinarian or poison control tells you to.

How Sago Palm Poisoning Develops

Stomach signs can start within hours. Liver damage can follow over the next few days.

15 minutes to 12 hours

Early signs

  • Vomiting, sometimes bloody
  • Drooling, diarrhea
  • Not eating, tiredness
1 to 3 days

Liver injury

  • Yellow gums or eyes
  • Bruising or bleeding
  • Dark stool
Severe cases

Life-threatening

  • Wobbliness, seizures
  • Liver failure, death
Sago palms are popular houseplants and are common outdoors in warm states. Seeds can look like toys or treats to a dog.

Common Questions

How much sago palm is toxic to a dog?

There is no safe amount. The ASPCA warns that one or two seeds can be fatal, and leaves and roots are toxic too. Go to a vet after any chewing.

My dog chewed a sago palm leaf but seems fine. Can I wait?

No. Signs can take hours to start, and liver damage can follow days later. Early decontamination and liver protection give the best chance.

Are other palms dangerous?

Coontie and cardboard palm are cycads like sago palm and are just as dangerous. Many true palms are not, but plants are easy to misidentify, so bring a photo and call poison control.
