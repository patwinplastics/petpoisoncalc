Did Your Dog or Cat Eat ADHD Medication? Check the Risk.

Estimate the dose for your pet’s size after eating a stimulant ADHD pill, using the mg on the prescription label.

Last reviewed November 27, 2026 by PetPoisonCalc against the sources listed below.

Stimulant ADHD medicines act fast in pets, and even part of one pill can cause signs in a small dog.

Amphetamine products have caused restlessness in dogs at under 0.1 mg per kg, which is less than one small tablet for most dogs. Signs can start within 15 to 30 minutes and last a day or more. Cats have no established cutoff. This tool cannot call any dose safe. Contact a veterinarian or animal poison control now.

Emergency Vet or Poison Control: Which Do I Call?

Because signs can start within minutes, vets often treat the signs directly. Do not give your pet any sedative or calming medicine from home.

Go Straight to an Emergency Vet If…

  • Your pet is pacing, agitated, trembling, or very hot
  • Your pet has a seizure or collapses
  • Your pet is a cat, or the result below says Emergency
  • Poison control or your vet has told you to come in

Call Poison Control First If…

  • You just found the chewed bottle and your pet seems normal
  • You are unsure how many pills are missing
  • You want to know how long to watch
  • You want a case number to hand to your vet
Do not try to make your pet vomit at home unless a veterinarian or poison control tells you to.

How Stimulant Poisoning Develops

Immediate-release pills act within about 30 minutes. Extended-release pills and patches can start later and last longer.

15 minutes to a few hours

Early signs

  • Restlessness, pacing, circling
  • Dilated pupils, panting
  • Vocalizing, agitation
Next hours

Getting worse

  • Fast heart rate, high blood pressure
  • Overheating
  • Tremors
Severe cases

Life-threatening

  • Seizures
  • Collapse
24 to 72 hours

Duration

  • Signs can last this long with extended-release forms
Cats may sit very still and stare instead of acting hyper, but their heart rate and temperature can still rise.

Published Data Behind These Estimates

These figures come from the Merck Veterinary Manual chapter written with Pet Poison Helpline. Doses are mg of drug per kg of the dog’s weight.

Dose (dogs) Reported effect
0.09 mg/kg amphetamine Restlessness and agitation reported
0.2 mg/kg amphetamine Overheating and fast heart rate reported
0.7 mg/kg amphetamine Tremors reported
1.3 mg/kg amphetamine Seizures reported
0.3 mg/kg methylphenidate Overheating and fast heart rate reported
Cats No established cutoff

How this calculator sorts results: amphetamine at 0.09 mg/kg and above, or methylphenidate at 0.3 mg/kg and above, = Emergency; lower doses = Urgent. Any amount for a cat, any other or unknown ADHD medicine, or any pet showing signs, = Emergency. There is no lower tier.

Common Questions

How much Adderall is toxic to a dog?

Restlessness has been reported at about 0.09 mg of amphetamine per kg. For a 40 lb (18 kg) dog that is under 2 mg, so a single 5 mg tablet is already above it. Seizures have been reported at 1.3 mg/kg.

My dog ate a Vyvanse capsule. Is it different?

Vyvanse is a form of amphetamine the body converts after swallowing. This page counts the full mg on the label, which may overestimate a little but keeps the result cautious. Call poison control either way.

What about non-stimulant ADHD medicines?

Non-stimulant medicines work differently and are not covered by this dose math. Treat any amount as an emergency and call poison control.
