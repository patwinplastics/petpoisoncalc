Estimate the elemental iron dose for your pet’s size from the label on iron pills, prenatal vitamins, or multivitamins.

Last reviewed November 27, 2026 by PetPoisonCalc against the sources listed below.

Iron can burn the stomach lining and, at high doses, damage the liver. Pets can seem to get better and then crash.

Iron pills, prenatal vitamins, and some multivitamins are common causes of poisoning. Vomiting usually starts within 6 hours, and serious cases can look better for a while before getting worse. This tool estimates relative urgency from published data. It cannot rule out risk; contact a veterinarian or animal poison control after any ingestion.

Emergency Vet or Poison Control: Which Do I Call?

Activated charcoal does not bind iron, so the first steps are usually making your pet vomit if it was recent, stomach protectants, and bloodwork at 4 to 6 hours.

Go Straight to an Emergency Vet If…

  • Your pet has vomited blood or has bloody or black stool
  • Your pet is weak, pale, or collapsing
  • The result below says Emergency
  • Poison control or your vet has told you to come in

Call Poison Control First If…

  • Your pet just ate the pills and seems normal
  • You are unsure how many are missing
  • You want to know whether to come in for bloodwork
  • You want a case number to hand to your vet
Do not try to make your pet vomit at home unless a veterinarian or poison control tells you to.

How Iron Poisoning Develops

Iron poisoning often comes in waves. A quiet period in the middle does not mean your pet is safe.

0 to 6 hours

Stomach injury

  • Vomiting, diarrhea, sometimes bloody
  • Belly pain
  • Tiredness
6 to 24 hours

Apparent recovery

  • Pet may seem better
  • Iron is still moving into the organs
12 to 96 hours

Serious cases

  • Vomiting returns, shock
  • Liver injury, bleeding problems
2 to 6 weeks

Late effects

  • Scarring that narrows the stomach or gut
Oxygen absorber packets from jerky and snacks, and some hand warmers, also contain iron. Call poison control if your pet chewed one.

Published Data Behind These Estimates

These figures are for elemental iron, the amount listed as “Iron” on Supplement Facts. They come from the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center and the Merck Veterinary Manual.

Elemental iron dose Reported effect
under 20 mg/kg Toxic effects not expected in healthy dogs and cats
20 to 60 mg/kg Mild stomach and gut signs possible
over 50 mg/kg Merck: making the pet vomit and stomach protectants generally warranted
over 60 mg/kg Potentially serious: stomach bleeding, acidosis, liver injury
100 to 200 mg/kg Potentially lethal without treatment

How this calculator sorts results: under 20 mg/kg = contact a professional now; 20 to 60 mg/kg = Urgent; over 60 mg/kg = Emergency. Bloody vomit or stool, or weakness, = Emergency at any dose.

Common Questions

How much iron is toxic to a dog?

Under 20 mg of elemental iron per kg is not expected to cause toxic effects in a healthy dog. From 20 to 60 mg/kg, stomach upset is possible, and above 60 mg/kg serious poisoning can develop. A common 325 mg ferrous sulfate tablet has about 65 mg of elemental iron.

What is elemental iron?

Iron in pills is bound to a salt such as ferrous sulfate. Only part of that weight is iron. The “Iron” line on Supplement Facts already gives the elemental amount, so use it when you can.

My dog seems fine after eating iron pills. Can I wait?

Call poison control or your vet now. Iron poisoning can look like it is getting better before the serious stage begins, and treatment works best early.
