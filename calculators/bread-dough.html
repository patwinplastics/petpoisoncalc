Did Your Dog or Cat Eat Raw Bread Dough? Check What to Do.

Raw dough made with yeast keeps rising in the stomach and turns sugar into alcohol. Tell us what happened to get your next step.

Last reviewed November 27, 2026 by PetPoisonCalc against the sources listed below.

There is no known safe amount of raw yeast dough. Any amount is treated as an emergency.

A pet’s warm stomach works like a proofing oven. The dough can swell and stretch the stomach, and the yeast makes alcohol that is absorbed into the blood. Veterinary sources list no known toxic dose, so this checker does not do dose math. Call poison control or an emergency vet now.

Emergency Vet or Poison Control: Which Do I Call?

A vet may try to bring the dough back up if it was eaten recently and your pet is acting normally. Dough is sticky, so this does not always work, and some pets need more care.

Go Straight to an Emergency Vet If…

  • The belly looks swollen or feels tight
  • Your pet is retching but nothing comes up
  • Your pet is wobbly, very sleepy, or weak
  • Your dog is a deep-chested breed, which has a higher bloat risk

Call Poison Control First If…

  • Your pet just ate the dough and seems normal
  • You are unsure how much was eaten
  • You want to know whether to go in right away
  • You want a case number to hand to your vet
Do not try to make your pet vomit at home unless a veterinarian or poison control tells you to.

How Raw Dough Poisoning Develops

Signs can start within an hour or two and get worse as the dough rises and alcohol builds up.

First hours

Stomach signs

  • Retching or unproductive vomiting
  • Swollen, painful belly
  • Restlessness
Next hours

Alcohol signs

  • Wobbly walking, confusion
  • Sleepiness, weakness
  • Low blood sugar, low body temperature
Severe cases

Life-threatening

  • Collapse, seizures, or coma
  • Stomach twisting in some dogs
Baked bread does not rise in the stomach. If the dough had raisins, chocolate, garlic, or xylitol, check those calculators too.

Common Questions

How much raw dough is dangerous for a dog?

No safe amount has been established. Even a small piece can keep rising and make alcohol, and small pets are affected by less. Call poison control or an emergency vet after any amount.

My dog ate pizza dough. What should I do?

Call poison control or an emergency vet now. Do not give food, and do not make your dog vomit unless you are told to. Watch for a swollen belly, retching, or wobbliness on the way.

Is baked bread a problem?

Baked bread has no live yeast, so it does not rise or make alcohol. Check for raisins, chocolate, garlic, onion, or xylitol in the ingredients, and call your vet if your pet ate a lot.
