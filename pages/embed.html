Rules

  • Keep the credit link visible, outside the iframe, with the anchor text "Pet poison calculator by PetPoisonCalc".
  • Do not add rel="nofollow" to that link.
  • Do not put ads inside the frame.
  • The result is an estimate. It never calls an amount safe. Visitors should call a vet or poison control.

The embed does not set cookies and does not load analytics. See the privacy policy. Questions about embedding go through the contact page. That address is not an emergency line.

PetPoisonCalc provides general risk estimates for educational purposes only and is not a diagnosis or a substitute for veterinary care. Always contact a veterinarian or an animal poison control center after any suspected toxic ingestion.
