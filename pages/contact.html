Contact PetPoisonCalc

Corrections, sourcing questions, and calculator suggestions are welcome. This page is not an emergency contact line.

If your pet is currently in danger, do not wait for an email reply.

Call the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426-4435 or the Pet Poison Helpline at (855) 764-7661, or contact your veterinarian directly. This contact form and email address are reviewed periodically and are not monitored for emergencies.

How to reach us

For non-urgent questions, corrections, or suggestions, email contact@petpoisoncalc.com. We aim to respond to sourcing corrections and factual questions within a few business days.

What is useful to send us

Corrections

  • A published veterinary source that contradicts a threshold used on a calculator
  • A broken link or an outdated phone number for a poison control resource
  • A factual error in symptom descriptions or timelines

Suggestions

  • Another common ingestion hazard you think deserves a dedicated calculator
  • Feedback on whether a result was confusing or hard to act on
  • Requests to extend an existing calculator to cover cats or other species

What we cannot help with

We cannot provide individualized medical advice about your specific pet, confirm whether your pet is safe, or replace an evaluation by a veterinarian or animal poison control center. Any message describing an active or recent ingestion will be answered with a reminder to contact emergency veterinary services, not with medical guidance.
