Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated October 20, 2026. PetPoisonCalc is free to use. This page explains, in plain English, how the site earns money and how we keep that separate from the safety information we give you.

The short version

PetPoisonCalc may include affiliate links. If you click one and then buy something or sign up for a service, we may earn a commission. This costs you nothing extra. As of the date above, the site does not contain any affiliate links. We do show display ads on some pages, but never on calculator pages.

How the site earns money

PetPoisonCalc has applied to, or may join, affiliate programs (for example through the Impact partner network). If we join, we may earn commissions from partners such as:

• Pet insurance providers
• Vet telehealth services (online vet visits)
• Pet product retailers, for example for prevention products like locking trash cans or pet-safe treats

We also show display ads served by third-party ad networks, such as Google AdSense, on some pages. Ads are kept off the calculator pages. See our Privacy Policy for how ad cookies work.

Money never affects results or advice

Calculator results and urgency levels are based only on the weight, substance, and amount you enter, compared against published veterinary toxicology data. No partner, advertiser, or commission has any influence on how a result is calculated or what urgency level is shown.

We do not accept payment to change, soften, or remove safety advice.

Poison control comes first, always

Affiliate links never appear in place of poison-control guidance. If your pet may have eaten something toxic, the first step we recommend is always to call a veterinarian or an animal poison control center: ASPCA Animal Poison Control at (888) 426-4435 or Pet Poison Helpline at (855) 764-7661. Any partner link is shown only as an optional extra, never as a substitute for that call.

How we label links

Any page with affiliate links will say so in a clear note at the top of the page and again next to the links, before you click. Affiliate links will be labeled as such. We only link to products or services we think may be genuinely useful to pet owners, and we will not claim to have personally tested a product unless we have. You should always make your own decision.

Questions

If you have questions about this disclosure, use our contact page. This page may be updated as partnerships change, and the date at the top reflects the latest update.

PetPoisonCalc provides general risk estimates for educational purposes only and is not a diagnosis or a substitute for veterinary care. Always contact a veterinarian or an animal poison control center after any suspected toxic ingestion.
