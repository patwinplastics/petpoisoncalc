Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026. This policy explains what data PetPoisonCalc collects, how cookies and advertising work on this site, and what choices you have.

Overview

PetPoisonCalc does not require you to create an account, does not ask for your name or your pet's identity, and does not store the weight, substance, or amount you enter into any calculator on our servers. Calculator inputs are processed entirely in your browser and are not transmitted to PetPoisonCalc or saved anywhere once you leave or refresh the page.

This policy covers the data collected by third-party services used on this site: website analytics and, where enabled, advertising.

Analytics

PetPoisonCalc uses standard website analytics tools, such as Google Analytics and Google Search Console, to understand aggregate traffic patterns, such as which calculator pages are visited and which search queries bring visitors to the site. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to distinguish visitors and do not identify you personally. No information about you or your pet entered into a calculator is included in this data.

Advertising and cookies

PetPoisonCalc may display advertising served by third-party ad networks, including Google AdSense. These networks and their partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites, and to measure ad performance. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies through their settings, though this may affect how some parts of the site display.

This site does not currently sell personal information, and any advertising cookies present are used solely for standard ad delivery and measurement by our network partners, not for any purpose specific to PetPoisonCalc.

Third-party links

PetPoisonCalc links to external resources, including the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center, the Pet Poison Helpline, and various veterinary and toxicology references cited on calculator pages. This privacy policy does not apply to those external sites, and we encourage you to review their own privacy practices.

Your choices and regional rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) to access, delete, or restrict the use of data collected about you by analytics or advertising partners. Because PetPoisonCalc itself does not collect or store personal data beyond standard analytics and advertising cookies, most such requests should be directed to the relevant third-party provider, such as Google, using the opt-out links above. For questions specific to this site, use the contact page.

Children's privacy

PetPoisonCalc is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The site's calculators can be used by anyone without providing any personal or identifying information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically as the site adds new features or advertising partners. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
