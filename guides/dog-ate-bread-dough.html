My Dog or Cat Ate Raw Bread Dough: What to Do Now

Rising dough on the counter, a dropped piece of pizza dough, a jar of sourdough starter: raw yeast dough is one of the few foods that gets more dangerous after it is swallowed. Here is why, and what to do.

If your pet ate raw bread or pizza dough: ASPCA Animal Poison Control (888) 426-4435 Pet Poison Helpline (855) 764-7661 Find a 24/7 emergency vet near me Both hotlines are open 24/7. A consultation fee may apply. This guide is general information, not veterinary advice. Medical disclaimer.

If your dog or cat ate raw yeast dough of any kind, call an animal poison control center or an emergency vet now. There is no known safe amount, so any amount is treated as an emergency. Go straight in if the belly looks swollen, your pet is retching with nothing coming up, or is wobbly or very sleepy.

What to Do Right Now

  1. Take away any dough that is left, and do not give your pet food.
  2. Note what it was (bread, rolls, pizza dough, sourdough starter), roughly how much, and the time.
  3. Call ASPCA Animal Poison Control, Pet Poison Helpline, or an emergency vet now.
  4. Do not make your pet vomit unless a professional tells you to.
  5. Watch for a swollen belly, retching, wobbliness, or sleepiness on the way in.

Why Raw Dough Is Dangerous

Veterinary sources describe two problems. First, the dough keeps rising in the warm, moist stomach. It can stretch the stomach, cause pain and bloating, and in at-risk dogs trigger the stomach to twist. Second, the yeast keeps fermenting and makes alcohol, which is absorbed into the blood (Merck Veterinary Manual).

Merck notes that when pets die, it is usually from the effects of the alcohol. Pet Poison Helpline rates bread dough as moderate to severe in toxicity depending on how much was eaten. Neither source gives a safe amount, so the raw bread dough checker treats any amount as an emergency.

Raw dough made without yeast does not rise or make alcohol, but if you are not sure about the yeast, treat it as yeast dough. If the dough had raisins, chocolate, garlic, or xylitol in it, check those calculators too.

Dogs vs Cats: What Is Different

  • Who eats it: dogs, by far. Counter-surfing dogs reach dough left to rise, and some eat a whole batch.
  • Bloat: deep-chested dog breeds have a higher risk of a twisted stomach, so go in right away.
  • Cats: eat dough less often, but they are small, so the alcohol from even a small piece matters. The checker treats any amount as an emergency for cats too.
  • Size: small pets are affected by less dough.

Symptoms and Timeline

  • First hours: retching or vomiting with little coming up, a swollen or painful belly, and restlessness. Signs can start within an hour or two.
  • Next hours: alcohol signs: wobbly walking, confusion, sleepiness, weakness, low blood sugar, and low body temperature.
  • Severe cases: collapse, seizures, or coma, and stomach twisting in some dogs.

Go Straight to an Emergency Vet If

  • The belly looks swollen or feels tight
  • Your pet is retching but nothing comes up
  • Your pet is wobbly, very sleepy, or weak
  • Your dog is a deep-chested breed
  • Your pet is a cat or a small dog

What the Vet May Do

If the dough was eaten recently and your pet is acting normally, the vet may try to bring it back up. Dough is sticky, so this does not always work. Pets with signs may need treatment for the alcohol, such as support for blood sugar and body temperature, and close monitoring for bloat or a twisted stomach.

How to Prevent It

  • Let dough rise in a closed oven (turned off), a microwave, or a closed room, never on a low counter.
  • Keep sourdough starter in a sealed jar on a high shelf or in the fridge.
  • Put scraps of raw pizza and roll dough straight into a lidded bin.
  • Keep pets out of the kitchen while you bake, especially during holiday baking.

Frequently Asked Questions

My dog ate raw bread dough. What should I do?

Call poison control or an emergency vet now, even if your dog seems fine. Do not give food, and do not make your dog vomit unless you are told to. Watch for a swollen belly, retching, or wobbliness on the way.

How much raw dough is dangerous for a dog?

No safe amount has been established. Even a small piece can keep rising and make alcohol, and small pets are affected by less. Call poison control or an emergency vet after any amount.

My dog ate pizza dough. Is that the same?

Yes. Raw pizza dough is usually made with yeast, so it can rise in the stomach and make alcohol like bread dough. Treat it as an emergency and call now.

Is sourdough starter dangerous?

Yes. Sourdough starter is a live yeast culture, so it carries the same rising and alcohol risks as raw dough. Call poison control or an emergency vet.

Is baked bread a problem?

Baked bread has no live yeast, so it does not rise or make alcohol. Check for raisins, chocolate, garlic, onion, or xylitol in the ingredients, and call your vet if your pet ate a lot.
