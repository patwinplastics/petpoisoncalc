Pet Ingestion Risk Calculators

Free tools that estimate how urgently your dog or cat needs veterinary attention after eating something toxic, based on weight and amount consumed. Built from published veterinary toxicology data.

Each calculator is labeled for the species it covers. Cat-specific tools now include lilies and acetaminophen. If your cat ate something not listed here, call ASPCA Animal Poison Control at (888) 426-4435 or Pet Poison Helpline at (855) 764-7661 right away.

No calculator on this site replaces professional care.

Every result is an urgency estimate based on published case data, not a diagnosis. For any suspected toxic ingestion, contact a veterinarian or an animal poison control center immediately, regardless of what a calculator shows.

Why we built this

We are pet owners too. When a dog or cat gets into something it should not, the first few minutes are scary and confusing, and most search results are slow, vague, or buried under ads. We wanted one place that gives a fast, clear answer to two questions: how worried should I be, and what should I do right now?

Every calculator is built from published veterinary toxicology sources, and each page lists the sources it uses. PetPoisonCalc is not a substitute for a veterinarian. If your pet may have eaten something toxic, call your vet or a poison control line right away: ASPCA Animal Poison Control at (888) 426-4435 or Pet Poison Helpline at (855) 764-7661.

About these calculators

PetPoisonCalc estimates relative urgency using published veterinary toxicology data and known case reports. Results are not a diagnosis and cannot rule out risk at any dose. Every suspected ingestion should be evaluated by a veterinarian or an animal poison control center, regardless of what a calculator shows.
